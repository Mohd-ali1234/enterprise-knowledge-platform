# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## Expense Policy

Employees may expense meals up to $75 per day while travelling. Receipts are
required for any single expense over $25. Expense reports must be submitted
within 30 days of the trip ending. Elena Rossi manages the Finance team and
approves all reimbursements over $500.

## Remote Work

Northwind Labs supports hybrid work. Employees are expected in the Berlin office
on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Fully remote arrangements require written approval
from a director. Marcus Webb approves remote requests for the Platform team.

## Leave

Every employee receives 28 days of paid leave per year, plus public holidays in
their country of residence. Leave requests go to your line manager at least two
weeks in advance. Unused leave does not carry over past March 31.

## Equipment

New starters receive a laptop and a monitor. Hardware requests above $1,200 need
sign-off from Elena Rossi. Lost equipment must be reported to the IT helpdesk
within 24 hours.
