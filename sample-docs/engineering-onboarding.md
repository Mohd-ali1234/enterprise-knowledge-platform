# Engineering Onboarding

Welcome to engineering at Northwind Labs. Priya Sharma is the CTO and owns
technical strategy across the company.

## Teams

Marcus Webb manages the Platform team, which maintains the deployment pipeline
and the shared service libraries. The Platform team is based in Berlin.

Aisha Khan leads the Data team. The Data team owns the analytics warehouse and
the reporting pipelines, and works from Toronto.

Tom Okafor leads the Security team and reports to Priya Sharma.

## First week

1. Collect your laptop from the IT helpdesk.
2. Request access to the internal wiki and the deployment console.
3. Read the security policy before requesting production credentials.
4. Pair with your onboarding buddy on a starter ticket.

## Tech stack

Services are written in Python and TypeScript. PostgreSQL is the primary
datastore. Deployments run on Kubernetes. The Platform team maintains the CI
system, and all production changes require a reviewed pull request.
