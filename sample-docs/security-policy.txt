Northwind Labs Information Security Policy

1. Device security

All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before they are used for
company work. Screens must lock after five minutes of inactivity. Report a lost
or stolen device to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

2. Credentials

Passwords must be unique per service and stored in the company password
manager. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the deployment
console and the cloud provider. Never share credentials over chat or email.

3. Production access

Production credentials are granted per person and expire after 90 days.
Tom Okafor leads the Security team and approves all production access requests.
Access reviews happen quarterly.

4. Incident response

Report a suspected incident immediately to the Security team. The Security team
triages within one hour during business hours. Tom Okafor is the incident
commander for any severity one event, and notifies Priya Sharma within 30
minutes of confirming a breach.

5. Data handling

Customer data may not be copied to personal devices. Analytics extracts must be
anonymised before they leave the warehouse. The Data team owns the anonymisation
tooling.
