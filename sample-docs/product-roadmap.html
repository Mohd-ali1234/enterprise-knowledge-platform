Northwind Labs Product Roadmap

Project Atlas

Project Atlas replaces the legacy billing service with a metered usage engine. Marcus Webb owns Project Atlas and the Platform team delivers it. Atlas ships to general availability in Q3 and is the largest engineering commitment of the year.

Atlas depends on the analytics warehouse for usage aggregation, so the Data team provides the nightly rollup jobs.

Project Meridian

Project Meridian is the customer-facing analytics dashboard. Aisha Khan owns Project Meridian. A private beta opens in Q2 with ten design partners, and Meridian reaches general availability in Q4.

Security work

The Security team runs an external penetration test before the Atlas launch. Tom Okafor coordinates the engagement and signs off the remediation plan.

Out of scope this year

Mobile applications, on-premise deployment and the partner API are explicitly out of scope until the Atlas migration completes.
